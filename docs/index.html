Questions

Short answers. No subscription fine print.

What gets saved?

Posts you open, like, repost, reply to, or bookmark — plus careful timeline reads after stable focus (~3s). Not every post you scroll past.

Does it use the X API?

No. It works on the web UI only — injects Local History and reads on-page metadata for items you engage with.

Is there a mobile app?

Not yet. X History is for the desktop browser on x.com / twitter.com.

Will X layout changes break it?

Sometimes, temporarily. Fixes ship as extension updates — that’s the tradeoff of living inside someone else’s UI.

How many items are kept?

About 500 recent items. You can delete individuals or clear all from the History panel.

Is this the same as X’s History tab?

No. X’s History is bookmarks, likes, videos, and articles on your account. This extension is a local browse history of posts you opened or engaged with — stored on this device.
